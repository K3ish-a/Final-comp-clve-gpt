SACRAMENT OF HEALING

God's Healing
and Comfort

The Anointing of the Sick is the sacrament through which Christ offers spiritual and physical healing to those who are seriously ill or suffering. Through the laying on of hands and anointing with holy oil, the Church entrusts the sick to God's loving care.

This sacrament gives peace, hope, courage, and spiritual strength. It reminds those who are suffering that they are never alone and that God remains present with them through their difficulties.

God's healing presence brings peace and hope.

UNDERSTANDING ANOINTING OF THE SICK

What happens in the Anointing of the Sick?

Healing

The sacrament asks God to bring healing and strength to those who are sick. While physical healing may happen, the sacrament especially provides spiritual healing, peace, and courage during times of illness.

Strength

Through the anointing with holy oil, Christians receive spiritual strength and courage to face illness and suffering. It helps them place their trust in God during difficult moments.

Comfort

The Anointing of the Sick reminds those who are suffering that they are not alone. It brings comfort and hope by deepening their union with Christ and reminding them of God's loving presence.

Signs and Symbols of Anointing of the Sick.

The sacrament uses important signs that help us understand God's healing and loving presence.


Oil of the Sick — Symbolizes healing, strength, and God's grace.

Laying on of Hands — Represents prayer, blessing, and the Church's care for the sick person.

Prayer — Expresses faith in God's healing, mercy, and loving presence.

Priest — Ministers the sacrament on behalf of Christ and the Church.

WHY IT MATTERS

Anointing of the Sick and the Christian Life

The Anointing of the Sick is important because it reminds us of God's compassion for those who are suffering. It gives spiritual strength, peace, and courage while helping the sick person place their trust in Christ.

This sacrament also reminds the Christian community to care for people who are sick, elderly, or suffering. It calls us to follow Jesus' example by showing compassion, offering support, praying for others, and being present for those in need.
